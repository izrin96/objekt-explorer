# Offers and the trade page

Part of `apps/web/AGENTS.md`, which points here.

## Trade page

Its state lives in the pure `features/offers/next-step.ts` (the status card's state, its `primary` button, each side's status and each leg's chip), `exchange.ts` (a leg's status and warnings, and how The exchange groups, filters and finds legs) and `trade-clock.ts` (`timelineRows`, the one Timeline for every state), beside their tests: a new state starts there, then `next-step-banner.tsx` (the status card, which also holds the stepper) maps it to copy, worded in `next-step-text.ts` beside its test. The phone bar reads the same `primary`, so the card hides the buttons the bar carries. An open request to cancel (`trade.cancelRequest`) outranks every other running state as `theyAskCancel` (Decline and Accept, `primary: "decide"`) or `youAskCancel` (Withdraw); its dialogs live in `cancel-trade.tsx`, and the menu offers Ask to cancel only while Cancel trade is locked and no request is open.

## Held offer actions

Decline and Withdraw on an open offer wait `HOLD_MS` before they send. The hold lives in `stores/offer-holds.ts`, beside its timer, so it outlives the card; Undo (notice or card) calls `cancelOfferHold`, and a closed tab drops it. Pure parts are in `features/offers/hold.ts`.

## Offer builder

Make offer and Counter are the page `/trade/@<handle>/offer` (`routes/(container)/trade/@{$handle}/offer.tsx`), never a dialog. Every caller links through `offerLink` (`features/offers/offer-link.ts`, tested): `MakeOfferButton` as a link, `useOpenOffer()` from a menu item or a drawer, both passing the current href as `from`. The search says how the offer starts (a list or conversation, the row's or one post's matches, a near pick, `counter`, a Market listing's `ask`), never the sender's picks; `requestOf` rebuilds the `OfferRequest` from it, and the loader reads a counter's offer (`offer.views`, refused unless `actions` has `counter`) checks that a conversation is the viewer's with this person, and starts from the person (`withoutLists`) when a list the search names isn't theirs. `partnerHandle` gives chat and the trade page a handle: a post card's, else the address, since a nickname can be shared.

`OfferPage` (`offer-builder.tsx`) lays the page out through `builder/layout.tsx`: `BuilderGrid`, the title in `BuilderTitle`, and `BuilderBody`'s slots (`sides`, `details`, `hint`, `cancel`, `send`, `picker`), from `lg` the title over a sticky column beside the sides (the title scrolls away, so the DOM reads title, status line, sides, details at every width), and below `lg` one column with a bottom bar. A request with `matches` opens through `MatchesBuilder`: it draws the title and one region for the matches line (`role="status"`, `STATUS_AREA`, above the sides; each side keeps its own banner for blocked picks) that stays mounted from the first frame to the leaving, reading "Adding your matches…", then the line or the failure; the body beside it changes from placeholders to `BuilderForm`, which portals its line (`builder/matches-line.tsx`, with Remove matches and Undo) into that region, so a screen reader hears every change in one node. A request without `matches` has no region. `BuilderForm` and `BuilderRefused` never draw the title: their caller does. The picker (`offer-picker.tsx`) is a sheet as wide as the main area. The partner's offer rules come with the theirs candidates (`partnerRules`) and `BuilderForm` applies them: a rule line on each side (`SideColumn`'s `rule`), You give's match switch locked on where `giveRule` (`builder/post-choice.ts`) says the rule filters (`MineGrid`'s `wantedOnly`, kept to the post's own want list through `wantList`, or every want list with no post), and, for a partner who takes asks from one post (`partnerRules.onePost`, their Let people ask from more than one post off), the header's Offer on choice over the partner's lists on Trade plus No post (`postSlug`: what the sender chose, else `openPost`, which is `start`, the matches' post, `to` when it is one of those lists, the first prefilled get pick's list, then their first list). You get's picker is fixed to it (`TheirsGrid`'s `post`, no List choice), its button reads "Add from {post}", `reasonOf` marks a get pick on another list or under No post, and the choice is sent as `post`. The decline nudge and the Messages notice (`features/offers/wanted-rule.ts`) keep their answers in `useNoticesStore`; the nudge marks itself on showing, since a toast's `onClose` fires on its timeout as well.

Leaving goes through `useBlocker` on `holdsDraft` (`builder/draft.ts`), with the browser's prompt on a reload; a sent offer skips it and replaces the page with the conversation. Back and Cancel (`useLeaveOffer`) step back through history when the page was opened in the app (`offerBack` in the history state, which the handle redirect keeps), so a feed comes back scrolled, else replace it with `from` or the person page. The control that opened the page takes focus again through the one-shot opener in `features/offers/return-focus.ts`: a Make offer link by its start, chat's Add to message as `chat-add:<id>`; a sent offer forgets it. Make offer links never preload, since each preload spends the feed's rate limit. For a person with no linked Cosmo profile there is no offer page, so the row's Make offer is `NoProfileOffer` (`features/trade/trade-actions.tsx`, focusable and `aria-disabled`, its reason toasted) and a post's is a disabled `ReasonItem`.

Make offer for this objekt starts from one collection of another person's have or sale strip: `PostBlock` hands the drawer an `ObjektAsk` (`features/trade/near.ts`) through `OpenObjekt`'s third argument, and `ObjektOfferNote` draws it in the drawer's `note` slot unless a near match's line takes it. Its `ask` names no copy, so the form resolves the get side through `resolveGetAsks` (`builder/draft.ts`, tested) over the partner's candidates: an any-copy entry on the ask's list keeps the ask, else the first copy there that can be sent stands in (`replaces`), else the ask stays and shows as one that can't be sent. Send waits while the candidates load. `holdsDraft` compares the unresolved picks; the get picker gets the side as shown and drops `replaces` on Done.
