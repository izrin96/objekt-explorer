# Offers and the trade page

Part of `apps/web/AGENTS.md`, which points here.

## Trade page

Its state lives in the pure `features/offers/next-step.ts` (the status card's state, its `primary` button, each side's status and each leg's chip), `exchange.ts` (a leg's status and warnings, and how The exchange groups, filters and finds legs) and `trade-clock.ts` (`timelineRows`, the one Timeline for every state), beside their tests: a new state starts there, then `next-step-banner.tsx` (the status card, which also holds the stepper) maps it to copy, worded in `next-step-text.ts` beside its test. The phone bar reads the same `primary`, so the card hides the buttons the bar carries. An open request to cancel (`trade.cancelRequest`) outranks every other running state as `theyAskCancel` (Decline and Accept, `primary: "decide"`) or `youAskCancel` (Withdraw); its dialogs live in `cancel-trade.tsx`, and the menu offers Ask to cancel only while Cancel trade is locked and no request is open. Under You get, `StillWanted` (`still-wanted.tsx`, lines from the pure `still-wanted-lines.ts`) lists each received collection that is still on one of the viewer's want lists with Remove objekts I get in a trade off (`leg.wantedOn`), with Remove and Always remove from this list (`list.dropReceived`). The exchange (`trade-exchange.tsx`, `exchange-group.tsx`) draws every leg, with both sides rendered at every width, so a trade holds up to twice `OFFER_SIDE_LIMIT` tiles; virtualise both grids before the limit rises again.

## Held offer actions

Decline and Withdraw on an open offer wait `HOLD_MS` before they send. The hold lives in `stores/offer-holds.ts`, beside its timer, so it outlives the card; Undo (notice or card) calls `cancelOfferHold`, and a closed tab drops it. Pure parts are in `features/offers/hold.ts`.

Accept sends at once, except on a lopsided offer (`isLopsided` in `features/offers/lopsided.ts`: both sides hold objekts and one holds at least `LOPSIDED_RATIO` times the other), where `OfferBody` first opens a confirmation that names both counts and offers Counter. The rule is the browser's alone: the server accepts either way.

## Offer builder

Make offer and Counter are the page `/trade/@<handle>/offer` (`routes/(container)/trade/@{$handle}/offer.tsx`), never a dialog. Every caller links through `offerLink` (`features/offers/offer-link.ts`, tested): `MakeOfferButton` as a link, `useOpenOffer()` from a menu item or a drawer, both passing the current href as `from`. The search says how the offer starts (a list or conversation, the row's or one post's matches, a near pick, `counter`, a Market listing's `ask`), never the sender's picks; `requestOf` rebuilds the `OfferRequest` from it, and the loader reads a counter's offer (`offer.views`, refused unless `actions` has `counter`) checks that a conversation is the viewer's with this person, and starts from the person (`withoutLists`) when a list the search names isn't theirs. `partnerHandle` gives chat and the trade page a handle: a post card's, else the address, since a nickname can be shared.

`OfferPage` (`offer-builder.tsx`) lays the page out through `builder/layout.tsx`: `BuilderGrid`, the title in `BuilderTitle`, and `BuilderBody`'s slots (`sides`, `details`, `hint`, `cancel`, `send`, `picker`), from `lg` the title over a sticky column beside the sides (the title scrolls away, so the DOM reads title, status line, sides, details at every width), and below `lg` one column with a bottom bar. A request with `matches` opens through `MatchesBuilder`: it draws the title and one region for the matches line (`role="status"`, `STATUS_AREA`, above the sides; each side keeps its own banner for blocked picks) that stays mounted from the first frame to the leaving, reading "Adding your matches…", then the line or the failure; the body beside it changes from placeholders to `BuilderForm`, which portals its line (`builder/matches-line.tsx`, with Remove matches and Undo) into that region, so a screen reader hears every change in one node. A request without `matches` has no region. `BuilderForm` and `BuilderRefused` never draw the title: their caller does. The picker (`offer-picker.tsx`) is a sheet as wide as the main area. The partner's offer rules come with the theirs candidates (`partnerRules`) and `BuilderForm` applies them: a rule line on each side (`SideColumn`'s `rule`), You give's match switch locked on where `giveRule` (`builder/post-choice.ts`) says the rule filters (`MineGrid`'s `wantedOnly`, kept to the post's own want list through `wantList`, or every want list with no post), and, for a partner who takes asks from one post (`partnerRules.onePost`, their Let people ask from more than one post off), the header's Offer on choice over the partner's lists on Trade plus No post (`postSlug`: what the sender chose, else `openPost`, which is `start`, the matches' post, `to` when it is one of those lists, the first prefilled get pick's list, then their first list). You get's picker is fixed to it (`TheirsGrid`'s `post`, no List choice), its button reads "Add from {post}", `reasonOf` marks a get pick on another list or under No post, and the choice is sent as `post`. A decline shows at most one notice, chosen by `declineNudge` (`features/offers/decline-nudge.ts`, tested): Only offers with what I want (`wanted-rule.ts`) before Let people ask from more than one post (`one-post-rule.ts`). Each keeps its answer in `useNoticesStore` and marks itself on showing, since a toast's `onClose` fires on its timeout as well.

The picker draws one tile per collection: `groupItems` (`copy-groups.ts`, pure and tested, as are `tileOf` and `anyControlOf`) folds a section's items into `CopyGroup`s, and `CandidateTile` takes the group. A lone copy toggles, an any item with only hidden copies is a count, and the rest open `SerialDialog` (`serial-dialog.tsx`), nested in the sheet; `OfferPicker` owns the one open group, and the tile takes focus back on close. The dialog edits a draft: Cancel, Escape and a click outside drop it, and its confirm writes through `applyGroup`, which replaces only the group's own copies and its any pick, so picks elsewhere on the side stay. The any control's top is `anyTop` (`quantity.ts`) everywhere: the picker's stepper, the dialog's count and, in the builder, `anyAvailable` and `pickProblem`'s "Only N available"; a second rule would let the picker offer a count the form blocks. `SideColumn` draws `byCollection(picks)`, a collection's cards together with the any pick first; `holdsDraft` keeps comparing the unordered picks. The sides draw every pick, unvirtualised, so from `lg` the page holds up to twice `OFFER_SIDE_LIMIT` tiles. The server never splits a collection across picker pages (`packages/api/docs/offers.md`), so a tile holds every copy of its collection, except under a serial search, where `candidateMatcher` keeps only the copies the `#n` terms name and drops the any item.

Leaving goes through `useBlocker` on `holdsDraft` (`builder/draft.ts`), with the browser's prompt on a reload; a sent offer skips it and replaces the page with the conversation. Back and Cancel (`useLeaveOffer`) step back through history when the page was opened in the app (`offerBack` in the history state, which the handle redirect keeps), so a feed comes back scrolled, else replace it with `from` or the person page. The control that opened the page takes focus again through the one-shot opener in `features/offers/return-focus.ts`: a Make offer link by its start, chat's Add to message as `chat-add:<id>`; a sent offer forgets it. Make offer links never preload, since each preload spends the feed's rate limit. For a person with no linked Cosmo profile there is no offer page, so the row's Make offer is `NoProfileOffer` (`features/trade/trade-actions.tsx`, focusable and `aria-disabled`, its reason toasted) and a post's is a disabled `ReasonItem`.

Make offer for this objekt starts from one collection of another person's have or sale strip: `PostBlock` hands the drawer an `ObjektAsk` (`features/trade/near.ts`) through `OpenObjekt`'s third argument, and `ObjektOfferNote` draws it in the drawer's `note` slot unless a near match's line takes it. Its `ask` names no copy, so the form resolves the get side through `resolveGetAsks` (`builder/draft.ts`, tested) over the partner's candidates: an any item on the ask's own list that `listedAsAny` (`schemas/offer.ts`: a whole-collection entry, or hidden copies) keeps the ask, while one that stands only beside copies the list names does not, else the first copy there that can be sent stands in (`replaces`), else the ask stays and shows as one that can't be sent. Send waits while the candidates load. `holdsDraft` compares the unresolved picks; the get picker gets the side as shown and drops `replaces` on Done.
