# Objekt Tracker

Trading Cosmo objekts between collectors: lists of what they have and want, offers made in chat, and the trades those offers turn into.

## Language

### Trading

**Deal**:
One offer of a conversation while it is open, its trade while it runs, or that trade after it ended while the viewer can still rate it. A conversation's row names its most urgent deal.
_Avoid_: active offer, conversation status

**Trade step**:
Whose move a running trade waits on, and what kind of move: answering a request to cancel, deciding on a different copy, sending, being stuck on an objekt that can't be sent, or waiting on the partner.
_Avoid_: trade state, trade status (the status is in progress, completed, failed or cancelled)
