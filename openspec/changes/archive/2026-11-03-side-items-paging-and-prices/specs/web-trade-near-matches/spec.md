## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Near matches in a list's sheet

A strip's "+N" sheet SHALL ring its near matches dashed, after the matches. When any are shown, a key SHALL name both rings: "On your lists" and "N more if you sell" (or swap, or buy). Its matching tab SHALL keep only matches; All SHALL hold the near matches. Near matches SHALL keep their place after the matches however many pages of the list have loaded.
